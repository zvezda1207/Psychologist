Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

1. General Provisions

This Personal Data Privacy Policy (hereinafter referred to as the Policy) applies to all information that the psychologist Artem (hereinafter referred to as the Operator) may obtain about the User while using the website and applying for psychological help.

Use of the website implies unconditional agreement of the User with this Policy and the conditions for processing their personal data specified therein. In case of disagreement with the terms of the Policy, the User should refrain from using the website and sending personal data.

2. Personal Data Processed

The Operator may collect and process the following personal data:

  • Name or pseudonym of the User;
  • Contact information (phone number, email address, messenger nicknames);
  • Information about the request the User makes (optional);
  • Technical data (IP address, cookies, browser and device data).

3. Purposes of Personal Data Processing

Personal data is processed for the following purposes:

  • Contacting the User to agree on the time and format of the consultation;
  • Providing psychological services;
  • Responding to questions and providing feedback;
  • Improving the website and the quality of services.

4. Principles of Personal Data Processing

Processing of personal data is carried out based on the following principles:

  • Legality and fair basis. Processing is carried out only with the User's consent or on other legal grounds provided for by the legislation of the Russian Federation.
  • Limitation of processing purposes. Data is processed only for the purposes specified in this Policy.
  • Data minimization. Only the data necessary to achieve a specific purpose is processed.
  • Accuracy and relevance. The Operator takes measures to keep personal data up to date.
  • Confidentiality. Data is not transferred to third parties without the User's consent, except in cases provided by law.

5. Psychological Confidentiality

Within the framework of psychological work, the Operator is guided by the principle of professional secrecy. All information obtained during consultations is strictly confidential and is not disclosed to third parties, except in cases provided for by the legislation of the Russian Federation (threat to life and health, obligations under the law).

Information obtained during psychological work is not used for marketing or other commercial purposes.

6. Legal Bases for Processing

The Operator processes the User's personal data on the basis of:

  • Federal Law No. 152-FZ of 27.07.2006 "On Personal Data";
  • The User's consent to the processing of personal data;
  • Contractual relations arising between the Operator and the User.

7. Procedure and Conditions for Processing

Processing of personal data is carried out using automation tools (website, email, messengers) and without their use (telephone conversations, face-to-face meetings).

The Operator takes necessary organizational and technical measures to protect personal data from unauthorized or accidental access, destruction, modification, blocking, copying, distribution, as well as from other unlawful actions of third parties.

8. Storage Periods and Data Destruction

Personal data is stored for the period necessary to achieve the processing purposes, or for the period established by the legislation of the Russian Federation. Upon achievement of the processing purposes or upon the User's request, the data is subject to destruction or depersonalization.

9. Rights of the Personal Data Subject

The User has the right to:

  • Receive information about the processing of their personal data;
  • Require clarification, blocking or destruction of their personal data;
  • Withdraw consent to the processing of personal data;
  • Appeal the actions or inaction of the Operator to the authorized body for the protection of the rights of personal data subjects (Roskomnadzor) or in court.

10. Transfer of Data to Third Parties

The Operator does not transfer personal data to third parties, except in cases where this is necessary for the provision of services (for example, the use of payment systems for online payment) or provided for by the legislation of the Russian Federation.

To send requests through the form on the website, a third-party service Formspree is used. Data transmitted through the form is processed in accordance with the Formspree Privacy Policy.

11. Cookies and Analytics

The website does not use third-party analytics systems (Google Analytics, Yandex.Metrika) and does not place advertising cookies. Technical cookies are used only for the correct operation of the website (for example, remembering the menu state).

12. Changes to the Policy

The Operator has the right to make changes to this Policy. The new version comes into force from the moment it is posted on the website. The User undertakes to independently monitor changes to the Policy.
