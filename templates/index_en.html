About Me

My name is Artem. I am a clinical psychologist and a specialist in Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT).

I am close to the language of art, cinema and literature - I often think in images and metaphors. This is naturally present in my work: not as a method, but as a way to talk about complex things simply and honestly.

Beyond my work, I love foreign languages, perfumery, film, and reading fiction.

In my office, there are no 'right' or 'wrong' words. You can be silent, cry, speak incoherently, repeat yourself - I don't interrupt or judge. We go at your pace.

How I Work

I work using ACT. It's not about "getting rid of anxiety", but about learning to live with it and doing what is important to you.

What is ACT?

Acceptance and Commitment Therapy - a therapy that teaches you to accept difficult experiences instead of fighting them, and to build a life according to your values, not rules of "how it should be".
